Questions, answered.

What is AI order entry?

AI order entry reads purchase orders that arrive by email, PDF, spreadsheet or WhatsApp, pulls out the customer, items, quantities and dates, matches them to your item numbers and creates the sales order in your ERP. Orders it is unsure about go to a person to confirm.

How much does it cost?

A fixed fee for a two-week audit, a fixed price for the build quoted in the audit’s spec before you commit, then a flat monthly fee to run it. Never per seat. The price depends mostly on how many order formats and systems are involved.

Which ERPs do you work with?

We build around the ERP you already run, such as Epicor Prophet 21 or Eclipse, Infor, NetSuite, Acumatica, SAP Business One, Microsoft Dynamics or Tally. If it accepts orders through an API, an import file or its database, we can connect to it.

Do we have to change our ERP or how customers order?

No. Customers keep sending orders the way they do today, and orders land in the ERP you already use. Nothing is migrated.

What happens when an order is unclear?

It is not guessed. The line is flagged for a person with the exact question to resolve, such as a unit of measure or an unknown part number, and the answer teaches the system for next time.

How long until it is running?

Two weeks for the audit and three to six weeks to build the first workflow, which runs side by side with your team on real orders before it goes live.

Who owns the software and the data?

You do. The code lives in your repository and your data stays yours. If you stop the monthly service, the system keeps running.

Is it only for distributors?

Distributors are our focus. The same approach works for manufacturers, contractors, logistics and service firms whose work arrives as email and ends as data entry.
