Questions, answered.

Does it work with TallyPrime?

Yes. Orders and receipts are posted into TallyPrime as vouchers, using your existing ledgers and stock items. You keep using Tally exactly as you do now.

Do our customers need a new app?

No. Customers keep ordering on WhatsApp, email or a phone call. The system reads what they already send.

Do we need the WhatsApp Business API?

For sending invoices and reminders automatically, yes. We set it up on your number. Meta charges a small fee per message, billed to you directly.
