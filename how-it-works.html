How an engagement works

We take one workflow or department at a time. You see a prototype working on your own examples before you commit to a build, and every output is approved by your team once it runs.

1. Workflow Sprint

2 weeks

From $1,500

The sprint answers one question with evidence: is this worth building, and should it be software, an automation or an agent?

  1. Week 1: map and measure. We sit with the people who do the work, walk through 20 to 30 real examples, and record each step, the systems involved, time per item and how often things go wrong.
  2. Week 2: prototype on your data. We build a working prototype and run it on examples it hasn't seen. You see where it is right, where it is wrong and why.

What you get

  • A map of the workflow, with today's hours and error rate
  • A working prototype and a recorded walkthrough of it on your examples
  • A fixed price and timeline for the full build
  • A plain recommendation, including "don't build this" when that's the answer

What we need from you

  • One person who owns the workflow, for about two hours a week
  • 20 to 30 real examples, anonymized if you prefer
  • Read-only access or exports from the systems involved

2. Build and launch

3 to 6 weeks, fixed price quoted after the sprint

  1. Connect. We connect the agent to your systems with its own credentials and only the permissions the workflow needs.
  2. Build the review step. Every output lands as a draft with its sources linked and exceptions flagged, wherever your team already works.
  3. Test on unseen cases. We hold back real examples and measure accuracy on them before anyone relies on the agent.
  4. Run side by side. For about a week the agent drafts while your team works as usual, so you can compare before go-live.
  5. Go live. Your team approves drafts instead of producing them. We train the people involved and document the workflow.

3. Run and improve

Monthly

  • Monitoring. We watch every run, and failures alert us before they reach your team.
  • A monthly report. Volume handled, time saved, corrections made and what changed.
  • Changes as you change. New price lists, new document types and new rules are part of the monthly fee.
  • Per-result pricing. Once volumes are steady, you can pay per quote, document or ticket instead of a flat fee.

You can stop at any point. The code, prompts, configuration and data are yours, and we hand them over.
