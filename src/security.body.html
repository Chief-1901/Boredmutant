How we handle your data and access

Agents that touch your inbox, CRM or documents need clear limits. Here is what we do on every engagement, and what we don't yet have.

Agreements first

  • We sign an NDA before any discovery call that involves your data.
  • We sign data-processing terms before we receive any examples or system access. We're happy to use yours.
  • We complete your security questionnaire as part of the sprint.

Access

  • Each agent gets its own credentials, never a person's login.
  • Permissions are limited to what the workflow needs. Read-only wherever the workflow allows.
  • You can revoke access at any time from your own admin settings.
  • Every action an agent takes is logged with the input it used and the person who approved it.

Data and models

  • Your data is used only to run your workflow.
  • We use model providers' business APIs, whose terms exclude training on customer data. We never put your data into consumer chat tools.
  • We keep only what the workflow needs, for as long as it needs it, and delete our copies when the engagement ends.
  • We choose models per workflow, and the system is built so models can be swapped without a rebuild.

Human review

  • Every output starts as a draft that a named person on your team approves.
  • Each drafted value links to its source, and anything that breaks your rules is flagged for a person.
  • Any move to automatic approval is your decision, made one step at a time, based on measured accuracy.

What we don't have yet

We're a small studio. We don't hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification. Instead, we work under your policies and agreements, answer your security questionnaire in full, and keep our scope narrow.

Patient health records and payment card data are out of scope unless the right agreements are in place first. In healthcare we work on administrative workflows only.
