What you can book

Three transaction patterns to start.

Aham launches with a deliberately limited set of booking patterns. Each one has its own readiness checks before it is offered.

Pattern 01

Fixed-price appointments

Services with a published price and a calendar: salons, clinics, classes. Pick a slot, approve the price, get a confirmed booking.

Pattern 02

Quote-required services

Repairs, renovations, custom work. Businesses return structured quotes with scope, price, timing and expiry — you compare and approve one.

Pattern 03

Consultations

Advice-first services: legal, financial, technical. Book time with a professional on stated terms, confirmed by the provider.

Not in scope

What Aham won't do

No holding your money, no payments on your behalf, no diagnosis or medical advice, no outreach to businesses that haven't joined the network.

Questions

Asked often.

Is Aham live yet?

Not yet. Aham is in early development in Hyderabad, and the flows shown on this site are illustrative. If you're a consumer or a service business who wants to shape the pilot, talk to us.

Do businesses need to install an app or change how they work?

No. Businesses participate through their existing messaging presence (starting with WhatsApp) plus a structured profile: services, areas, hours, pricing rules and an escalation path. Onboarding starts as a conversation, and the owner reviews everything before activation.

Who handles payment?

You do, directly with the provider, on terms you approved. Aham coordinates discovery, offers and booking state — it never holds your money or pays on your behalf.

Can the agent book something I didn't approve?

No. Deterministic software checks your preferences and spending mandates before anything can proceed, and a booking is only reported as confirmed after the provider confirms it through their own booking path.

Why Hyderabad first?

Starting in one city lets us get the transaction patterns right with real businesses before expanding. The architecture is designed to support more categories and geographies after validation.
