The network

A familiar chat on the surface. Clear rules underneath.

People use WhatsApp to describe what they need. Aham coordinates the request, business responses, approvals and booking state behind the conversation.

For service businesses

Make your services understandable to authorized agents.

A participating business configures its services, availability, pricing rules, booking terms and escalation path. Its agent can answer within those rules and hand uncertain cases to a person.

Business setup

Start with a conversation

Onboarding is designed to collect a structured business profile and service catalog conversationally, with owner review before public activation.

  • Services and areas
  • Hours and capacity
  • Pricing rules
  • Human escalation
Supported patterns, planned

Appointments, quotes and consultations

The first transaction patterns are fixed-price appointments, quote-required services and consultations. Each category needs its own readiness checks before it is offered.

  • Discover
  • Offer
  • Authorize
  • Book or escalate

Connecting a business WhatsApp account or calendar can require a secure provider handoff or browser-based authorization; setup is not guaranteed to happen entirely in chat.

Where the project stands

Building the transaction foundation first.

Aham is in early development. The repository contains the initial API, worker, shared schemas and transaction rules, PostgreSQL migrations, and a deterministic local demo.

Not production-ready: the local demo uses SQLite and mock offers/bookings. PostgreSQL is the planned production source of truth. WhatsApp integration code exists, but live customer bookings, business onboarding and production integrations are still future work.

WhatsApp-first

Consumers and business owners can begin with a familiar messaging channel. Secure integration steps may still need a handoff.

Real offers only

Availability and terms must come from the provider. Ambiguous or unsupported cases go to a person.

Permission before action

User mandates and business policies are checked in deterministic software before a transaction can proceed.

Hyderabad first

The architecture is intended to support more service categories and geographies, while the initial operational focus is Hyderabad.

A careful first release

A broad foundation, a deliberately limited launch.

Designed for the first release

Three reusable transaction patterns

Fixed-price appointments, quote-required services, and consultations give the network a controlled starting point. General services, auto repair and home repair may be piloted only after category checks.

Not an initial promise

No blanket automation or open-ended commitments

Healthcare is limited to administrative appointment handling after privacy and safety review. No diagnosis, medical advice, arbitrary business outreach, customer fund custody or unapproved payment handling.
