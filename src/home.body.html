Questions

What every firm owner asks first.

Is our client data safe?

Attachments never leave your inbox. The system tracks names, email addresses and document titles only. It never opens, stores or moves the files themselves. Everything sends through your own mailbox using your own credentials.

What if the AI writes something wrong to a client?

It can't, because it doesn't write. You approve three fixed templates once, in your firm's voice. The system only decides when to send them and when to stop. No generated text ever reaches a client.

What happens when a client replies with a question?

That client's chase pauses instantly and the thread goes to your staff, flagged for attention. The system only chases silence, so once a client has actually replied it steps back.

Do our clients need to learn new software?

No. They reply to your email with the file attached, the way they always have.

How long until it's running?

About a week from the call. Ten minutes of your time to hand over the list, one round of template approval, then two days in dry-run mode where you watch every email it would have sent before any real one goes out.

What if we want to stop?

Cancel any time, because it's month to month. Your clients were never on a platform and your data never left your systems, so nothing needs migrating. You go back to chasing manually.
