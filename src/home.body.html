Questions buyers ask

What does the Workflow Sprint cost, and what do we get?

It starts at $1,500 and runs for two weeks. You get a map of one workflow or department with time and error rates measured, a working prototype tested on your own data, and a fixed quote for the full build. If the workflow isn't a good fit for agents, we tell you during the sprint.

Do you use our data to train AI models?

No. We use model providers' business APIs, whose terms exclude training on customer data, and we never put your data into consumer chat tools. Your data is used only to run your workflow.

Which systems do you work with?

Usually the ones you already have: email, your CRM or ERP, accounting and practice software, shared drives and spreadsheets. If a system has an API or a reliable export, we can usually work with it. We confirm this during the sprint, before you commit to a build.

Will you replace our ERP or accounting system?

No. We build around it. Your ERP or accounting software stays the system of record, and the department software and agents we build read from it and write back to it.

What is a company brain?

A private assistant that answers questions from your documents, email and systems, with links to the sources. It respects existing permissions, so people only see answers drawn from what they can already access. Over time it becomes the shared memory your agents and workflows use.

What happens when the agent gets something wrong?

Nothing goes out without a person approving it, and every value links back to its source, so mistakes are caught at review. Corrections are logged and used to improve the workflow.

Who owns what you build?

You do: the code, prompts, configuration and data. If you stop working with us, we hand everything over and delete our copies of your data.

How is the work priced after launch?

The build is a fixed price, quoted at the end of the sprint. After launch, a monthly fee covers running, monitoring and changes. Once volumes are steady, we can price per result instead, such as per quote or per document.

Where are you based?

In Hyderabad, India. We work with firms in the US, UK and India, and schedule calls in your time zone.
